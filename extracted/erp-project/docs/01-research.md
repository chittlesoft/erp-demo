# Research Summary
Source: https://erpdrive.in/guide/manufacturing-erp-india.html

## Core modules (9) + extras (4)
Production Planning, BOM, Inventory, Quality Control, Purchase, Sales & CRM, GST Invoicing, Job Work, Reports & Analytics. Extras: Finance & Accounting, Service & Warranty, HR & Attendance, Dispatch & Logistics.

## India compliance
HSN-based GST rates; CGST/SGST/IGST by place of supply; e-way bills; job-work challans (Sec. 143); GSTR-1/3B-ready reports; ITC reconciliation. E-invoice (IRN/QR) not covered by the source — plan separately. OEM: lot traceability, PPAP, IATF 16949, SPC.

## Benchmarks
- Market price: Rs 500–3,000/user/month; implementation Rs 50k–3L.
- Go-live 6–8 weeks; 6 phases (discovery, config, migration, training, 1–2 wk parallel run, support).
- Claimed ROI: inventory -15–25%, on-time delivery 90–95%+, rejections -20–40%, 50–100+ hrs/month saved; payback 6–12 months (general claim).
- Pitfalls: big-bang rollout, dirty data, weak training, IT-only ownership.
